## Reuse note

The source photographer and license are also stored alongside each image in
Sanity Studio as `credit`, `license` and `sourceUrl` metadata; `altText`
describes the image for accessible use. When displaying a photo outside the
CMS, retain the author's attribution and follow the linked license terms.
In particular, CC BY-SA requires adaptations to be shared under the same or a
compatible license.
